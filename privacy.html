Privacy Policy: the White Rabbit Mind Channel app

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What this app is

"Channel" is a private tool used only by the owner of the White Rabbit Mind YouTube channel (youtube.com/@whiterabbitmind). It is not offered to the public.

What it does

It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own channel and to read that channel's own statistics and analytics.

What data it uses

It only accesses the channel owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's permission given on Google's sign in screen. It does not collect, store or process data about any other person.

What it stores

Sign in tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else.

Your choices

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the White Rabbit Mind YouTube channel.
